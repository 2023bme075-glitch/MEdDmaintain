WHY EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE MATTERS

Behind every dependable device is a maintenance process.

From basic diagnostic tools to complex imaging and life-support systems, medical devices must work correctly and be available when needed. Poor equipment management can delay diagnosis and treatment, while effective maintenance helps reduce downtime and support better care.

THE MAINTENANCE CYCLE

Keeping medical technology ready for care

A structured approach throughout the equipment lifecycle.

Installation and setup

Plan the installation, check power and environmental requirements, integrate devices where needed, and test performance before clinical use.

Preventive maintenance

Carry out scheduled inspections, cleaning, part replacement and software updates using manufacturer guidance and equipment usage needs.

Calibration and testing

Check and adjust device accuracy, verify safety features and document results so measurements and performance can be trusted.

Troubleshooting and repair

Identify root causes, diagnose hardware or software faults, repair safely and return equipment to service as quickly as possible.

THE IMPACT ON PATIENT CARE

Less downtime. More dependable care.

Well-managed equipment helps reduce interruptions to diagnosis and treatment. Regular maintenance can identify problems early, extend equipment service life and help clinical teams work with devices that are safe and ready for use.

  • Support patient and staff safety
  • Improve equipment availability and reliability
  • Keep maintenance history for traceability and audits
  • Help healthcare facilities use resources efficiently
